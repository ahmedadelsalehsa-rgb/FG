## 1. Story hook
A Critical defect passes its 24-hour SLA. The KPI turns red and a breach is raised. Who is told, and how fast?

## 2. Purpose & outcomes
After this chapter, the participant can:
- Define KPIs with formula, target, thresholds, owner and data source
- Read trends, not just snapshots
- Detect, classify, escalate and close breaches

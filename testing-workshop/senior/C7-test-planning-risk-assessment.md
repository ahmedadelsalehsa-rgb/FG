## 1. Story hook
With the strategy and gates agreed, the Test Manager writes the plan for R1.0. The plan starts by finding out what could go wrong.

## 2. Purpose & outcomes
After this chapter, the participant can:
- Run a product risk workshop and score risks
- Identify project risks with owners and triggers
- Write a Master Test Plan and level plans
- Obtain commitment to the plan

## 3. Concepts
Planned scope:
- Product risk identification & analysis (likelihood × impact)
- Project risk identification, responses, owners
- Master Test Plan contents (scope, approach, levels, estimates, organisation, schedule, environments, risks)
- Level test plans / sprint test plans
- Plan review and commitment (G2)

## 1. Story hook
Release R1.0 of the internal project is about to start. Before anyone writes a test, the team needs to know who does what, who decides, and which rules the organisation has committed to in its Test Policy.

## 2. Purpose & outcomes
After this chapter, the participant can:
- Draw the IT organisation and place the Test/QA function in it
- Explain the interfaces between QA and BA, Dev, PMO, Infra/Ops, Security, Release Mgmt and Service Desk
- Name the governance bodies (Steering Committee, CCB, CAB) and what each one decides
- Explain the purpose and contents of a Test Policy
- Trace a project rule back to a policy statement

## 3. Concepts
Planned scope:
- IT department map: BA, Dev, QA/Test, PMO, Infra/Ops, DBA, Security, Release Mgmt, Service Desk/Support
- QA function: centralised vs embedded vs hybrid; levels of test independence
- Test roles & job descriptions: Test Analyst, Technical TA, Automation Eng., Test Lead, Test Manager, Quality Gatekeeper
- Interfaces & hand-offs per department (inputs QA receives / outputs QA delivers)
- Governance bodies introduced: Steering Committee, CCB, CAB, Defect Triage (deep dive in B5)
- Release RACI
- **Test Policy:** Definition of testing & business objectives of testing
- **Test Policy:** Test process overview (standard test process)
- **Test Policy:** Test evaluation: how test quality and effectiveness are measured (link to KPIs)
- **Test Policy:** Quality level targets
- **Test Policy:** Approach to test process improvement
- **Test Policy:** Mandatory rules: gates, independence, change control, records retention
- **Test Policy:** Policy governance: owner, review cycle, approval

## 1. Story hook
A risk identified in planning comes true: the environment goes down for three hours. Risks have to be watched, re-scored and turned into issues.

## 2. Purpose & outcomes
After this chapter, the participant can:
- Monitor and re-assess product and project risks
- Convert materialised risks into issues and actions
- Report risk coverage and residual risk at gates

## 3. Concepts
Planned scope:
- Risk review cadence (weekly, at gates, after CR/CAPA)
- Risk re-scoring and trend
- Risk → issue conversion and escalation
- Risk coverage (K4) and residual risk
- Risk acceptance at Conditional Go

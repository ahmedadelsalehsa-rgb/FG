## 1. Story hook
A week after release a customer reports wrong VAT rounding. Fixing it is not enough. The team has to find out why it escaped.

## 2. Purpose & outcomes
After this chapter, the participant can:
- Run 5 Whys, Fishbone and Pareto analyses
- Distinguish symptom, direct cause and root cause
- Use defect root-cause data across a release

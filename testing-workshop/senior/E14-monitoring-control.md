## 1. Story hook
Week 2 of execution: progress is behind and the Critical defect is still open. The Test Lead has to see it early, report it honestly and act.

## 2. Purpose & outcomes
After this chapter, the participant can:
- Monitor progress and product quality against the plan
- Produce status reports for different audiences
- Apply suspension/resumption criteria and control actions

## 3. Concepts
Planned scope:
- Monitoring planning parameters: effort, schedule, scope, environment, commitments, stakeholders
- Monitoring product quality: entry/exit criteria, defects, risks
- Progress reviews and milestone reviews
- Status reporting: daily / weekly / milestone; audience-specific
- Suspension & resumption criteria
- Control actions and corrective actions to closure
